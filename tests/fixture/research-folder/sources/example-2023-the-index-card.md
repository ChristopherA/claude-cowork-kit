BRIEF

An invented post for the fixture, arguing that a note small enough for one card is easier to place and to find again.

SHORT ABSTRACT

The post compares notes kept on index cards with notes kept in long documents. It argues that the size limit of a card forces one claim per note. It concludes that a digital note gains the same discipline from a short brief at its top, written before the body and kept to a single sentence.

KEY POINTS

- **The card's limit is the method:** one claim fits, so each note has one place.
- **A brief does the same work:** a short summary at the top keeps a digital note to one claim.

KEY QUOTES

    > "A card that holds one claim can only be filed in one place." (p. 1)

WHY SAVED

It argues for the brief at the top of every note, which I use.
