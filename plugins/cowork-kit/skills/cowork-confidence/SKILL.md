# Confidence

Stop and say, the way a careful colleague would, what you actually know here and what you are guessing. Then let the reader decide whether to go on or dig. This is the kit's "say where you're uncertain, in the sentence where it matters" rule, made callable at a moment the reader chooses.

## What is being assessed

Find the live thing: the note being drafted, the filing decision in progress, the answer just given, the plan for a session. If the reader named it, use that. If not, take the most recent substantive thing you produced or proposed, and say that is what you are assessing.

## Say it in three parts, in prose

Not a table, not a score. Three short paragraphs:

**Confident about.** What you have seen directly: the file you read, the sentence in `map.md`, the source the reader handed you, the arithmetic you showed. Name the evidence for each item in the same sentence, so the reader can check it rather than trust it.

**Less confident about.** What you inferred, remembered from earlier in the conversation, or filled in because it seemed likely. Say which of those it is. If a claim came from memory of an earlier session rather than from a file, say so; at the desk you have no such memory, and the reader should know when you are working from the description alone.

**What would close the gap.** For each uncertain item, the one concrete thing that would settle it: a file to open, a question for the reader, a source to hand over, a check to run. Say which of those you can do now and which need the reader.

## Then ask

One question: proceed as is, close the gaps first, or stop here. Give your recommendation before asking, with the reason in a sentence. If the gaps are in something that will be written into the folder, recommend closing them first; a wrong note costs more than a delayed one.

## What this skill does not do

It does not hedge everything to look careful; an item goes under "confident" when the evidence is in hand. It does not invent uncertainty to seem humble, or invent certainty to seem useful. It does not proceed on its own after reporting.
